What we do

For prices and how long a style takes, call and ask. It depends on the length of the hair and the size of the braids.

  • Cornrows

    Braided flat to the scalp, in straight lines or set patterns, as fine or as thick as you want them.

  • Box braids

    Hair parted into sections and plaited down, with or without added hair.

  • Protective styles

    Styles that tuck your own hair away and keep it out of the weather while it grows.

  • Washing, cutting and styling

    The everyday work, in the same chair. Come in for a wash and a style without a braid appointment.

The salon is on the grounds of Xaba Guest Lodge, off Dune Route in Richards Bay – New. It is not inside the lodge building: it is the deep red shipping container standing on the sand, with SALON OPEN painted on the side in white.

If your map app takes you to the lodge, you are in the right place. Walk in through the grounds.

Hours

Open every day, from morning until 7:30 pm. Braiding takes hours rather than minutes, so if you are coming late in the day, call first and check there is time.
